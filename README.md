
# 📊 Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
An interactive Sales Performance Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, product trends, and salesperson contributions.

The project aims to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights that support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Monitor overall sales and profit performance.
- Analyze sales trends over time.
- Evaluate product and category performance.
- Compare regional sales performance.
- Measure individual salesperson contributions.
- Identify business opportunities and performance gaps.

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Overview
Provides a comprehensive summary of business performance through key performance indicators (KPIs), monthly sales trends, category analysis, regional performance, and payment method distribution.

### 2. Yearly Performance
Analyzes annual sales and profit performance, including Year-over-Year (YoY) growth and comparisons across years, categories, and regions.

### 3. Salesperson & Product Analysis
Evaluates salesperson performance and product contribution, highlighting sales, profit, top-performing employees, and best-selling products.
